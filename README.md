--> Amazon Sales Analysis – SQL Queries

--> Overview

This repository contains SQL queries designed to analyze an Amazon sales dataset and uncover key business insights related to revenue, pricing, discounts, customer behavior, and market performance. The objective is to translate raw transactional data into actionable insights using structured querying.

---

--> Objective

To identify the primary drivers of revenue and evaluate the impact of:

* Pricing strategy
* Discount effectiveness
* Product ratings
* Customer region
* Payment methods

---

--> Key Analysis Areas

* **Revenue Distribution:** Identified top-performing products and revenue concentration
* **Pricing Analysis:** Evaluated the relationship between price, demand, and revenue
* **Discount Impact:** Assessed whether discounts improve sales or reduce profitability
* **Rating Analysis:** Examined how customer ratings influence performance
* **Regional Insights:** Compared revenue and demand across customer regions
* **Payment Behavior:** Analyzed customer preferences across payment methods

---

--> Key Insights

* Revenue is moderately concentrated among top-performing products
* Higher-priced products drive significantly more revenue without impacting demand
* Discounts increase sales volume marginally but reduce overall revenue
* Ratings show limited direct impact on revenue performance
* Regional and payment method variations highlight customer behavior patterns

---

--> Business Recommendations

* Prioritize high-value products to maximize revenue
* Optimize discount strategies to avoid unnecessary revenue loss
* Leverage high-performing regions for targeted growth
* Align payment strategies with customer preferences
* Focus on value-driven positioning rather than aggressive pricing tactics

---

--> Tech Stack

* SQL (Data extraction and transformation)
* Relational dataset (structured transactional data)

---
--> Outcome

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used not just for querying data, but for driving business decisions through structured analysis and insight generation.

---
--> Final Note

The focus of this work is on combining technical querying with business understanding, ensuring that insights are meaningful, interpretable, and actionable.
